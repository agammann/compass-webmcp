# Compass pricing

Compass costs **$0 USD**. The public application and MIT-licensed source are available without an account, trial, subscription, payment method, or API key.

The complete local-first workspace, fictional Atlas demo, Context Packs, permission controls, ten page-side WebMCP tools, activity history, import, export, and supported undo operations are included for free.

Compass has no checkout, paid tier, subscription, marketplace, or in-product purchase.
